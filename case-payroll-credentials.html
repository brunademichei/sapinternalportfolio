SAP SuccessFactors Payroll USA · Enterprise web

Third-Party Credentials For Payroll

A clear, guided setup so customers can register the BSI/TaxFactory credentials that US payroll depends on, before they run any payroll activity.

My role
UX Designer (interaction & UI)
Product
SAP SuccessFactors Payroll USA
Platform
Enterprise web (SAP Fiori)
Focus
IA, interaction design, system patterns

Overview

SAP SuccessFactors Payroll USA is a highly complex enterprise product used to manage payroll operations for large-scale organizations, processing critical employee compensation data with strict compliance and regulatory requirements. Because of its complexity and high-impact nature, the product demands clarity, consistency, and precision in every user interaction.

I work closely with Product Managers, Development Leads, and Engineers to translate complex business requirements and user needs into clear, structured experiences. My role involves interpreting discovery insights and transforming them into user flows, interaction models, and high-fidelity UI aligned with SAP design guidelines and existing SuccessFactors patterns. I also take part in Agile ceremonies, bringing usability feedback and creating tickets when I spot usability problems, UI inconsistencies, or unexpected behaviors.

The requirement

This is the requirement I designed for:

The customer must log in and, before performing any activities (such as hiring), register their BSI credentials. Payroll depends on BSI for tax calculation, jurisdiction determination, and forms processing, and SFPAY cannot be used until this setup is complete. If a future integration needs credentials, they can be added here too. These settings are metadata-driven, so when a user logs in for the first time, the relevant data is already preconfigured.

The challenge

The credentials step is a hard gate: nothing else in payroll works until it is done. The design had to make a back-office, compliance-heavy task feel discoverable and trustworthy, with clear feedback about what was saved, what was active, and what still needed attention.

The flow I designed

The configuration lives inside the payroll Fine-Tuning area, reusing patterns customers already know. I designed clear states (Maintained and Configured), an activation toggle per credential version, and an activation log so administrators can trust and trace what happened.

Outcome

The result is a guided, discoverable setup that unblocks first-time logins and gives administrators confidence at every step: a predictable place to configure credentials, explicit status feedback as they move from Maintained to Configured, and an activation log for traceability, all consistent with existing SuccessFactors patterns.
