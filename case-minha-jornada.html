Product design · Journey mapping

Minha Jornada Platform

A centralized journey hub for AIESEC exchange participants and managers in Brazil, turning a fragmented operational process into a guided, transparent, end-to-end experience.

My role
UX/UI design, journey mapping
Context
AIESEC in Brazil
Users
Exchange participants & managers
Focus
IA, navigation, progress visibility

Overview

The “Minha Jornada” platform was designed to improve the experience of exchange participants (EPs) and managers throughout the AIESEC exchange journey within AIESEC in Brazil.

The primary challenge was the lack of visibility and structure across the end-to-end process. Participants often felt uncertain about their progress and next steps, while managers struggled to track engagement, communication, and overall journey status. Critical information was fragmented across spreadsheets, chat channels, and multiple tools, resulting in a disconnected and inconsistent experience.

The approach

To address this, the platform was designed as a centralized journey hub, letting EPs and managers follow each stage of the exchange process in a more structured, transparent, and intuitive way. The experience focused on improving information architecture, simplifying navigation, and providing clearer progress visibility throughout the user journey.

My contribution focused on UX/UI design, journey mapping, and experience structuring, with a strong emphasis on usability and engagement. The goal was to transform a fragmented operational process into a guided, supportive experience, helping users feel informed, oriented, and connected throughout the exchange lifecycle.

High-fidelity design

The final design speaks to two audiences: a welcoming landing experience for participants and a focused operational cockpit for managers. The longer screens scroll inside their frames.

From wireframes to high fidelity

The structure was defined first in low-fidelity, focusing on information architecture and flow before any visual styling.

Outcome

Minha Jornada replaced a fragmented, spreadsheet-driven process with a single, guided platform. Participants and managers now share one end-to-end view of the exchange journey, with the interface adapting to each role.

The design gave participants a clear, step-by-step path through their exchange, and gave managers an operational cockpit to track pipeline, compliance, and what needs attention, so the whole journey is visible in one place instead of scattered across tools.
