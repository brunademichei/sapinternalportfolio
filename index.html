My design process

User-centered and data-informed, grounded in research and testing, not assumptions.

  1. 01

    Discover

    Understand users, business goals, and constraints through interviews, surveys, and stakeholder conversations.

  2. 02

    Define

    Synthesize research into problems worth solving: journey maps, information architecture, and clear success criteria.

  3. 03

    Design

    Explore solutions through user flows, wireframes, and prototypes, leveraging AI tools to iterate quickly.

  4. 04

    Validate

    Test with users, refine with feedback, and partner with engineering to ship accessible, high-quality experiences.

About me

I'm a UX designer focused on enterprise software, where small design decisions ripple out to thousands of people doing real work every day. At SAP SuccessFactors Payroll, I design user flows, wireframes, mockups, and interactive prototypes that solve real problems and align with business goals.

I work as an embedded member of a cross-functional Scrum team, partnering closely with Product Managers and Engineers to validate feasibility and ship high-quality outcomes. I care deeply about accessibility, design-system consistency, and grounding decisions in research rather than assumptions.

Lately I've been designing AI-assisted experiences, including conversational flows and guided interactions, with a strong emphasis on Responsible AI: anticipating harms, embedding guardrails, and making AI reasoning transparent and trustworthy.
